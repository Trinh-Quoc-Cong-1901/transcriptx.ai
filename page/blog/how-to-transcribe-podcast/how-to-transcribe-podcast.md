# How to Transcribe a Podcast: Get a Transcript of Any Episode

> **Quick answer:** To transcribe podcast episodes, start with your app: Spotify and Apple Podcasts show read-along transcripts for many episodes. For text you can edit and save, paste the episode's YouTube link or upload the audio file to [TranscriptX Audio to Text](/audio-to-text/), then copy it or download it as .txt, .docx, .pdf, .srt or .vtt.

A podcast transcript is the spoken words of an episode as text. You might want one to find a quote without scrubbing through an hour of audio, to study an interview, to share notes with your team, or to publish alongside your own show.

There are three ways to transcribe a podcast, and the right one depends on where the episode lives and what you need to do with the text. This guide walks through each: the transcripts built into Spotify and Apple Podcasts, a YouTube link, and an audio file.

If you only need to read one passage, start with the app. If you need the full text in a document, subtitles or notes, go straight to the TranscriptX sections below.

## How to Get a Transcript of a Spotify Podcast

Spotify shows an auto-generated transcript for many podcast episodes. It appears inside the player, so you can read along as the episode plays.

1. **Open the episode** in the Spotify app and start playing it.
2. **Open the full player** by tapping the mini player at the bottom.
3. **Scroll down** below the playback controls. If the episode has a transcript, you will see it there.
4. **Tap the transcript** to open it full screen and follow along.

Not every episode has one. It depends on the show, the language and whether the creator has turned transcripts on.

The Spotify transcript is built for reading in the app, not for exporting. If you want a Spotify podcast transcript as a file, find the same episode on YouTube or get its audio file, then use one of the TranscriptX methods below.

## How to See the Transcript on Apple Podcasts

Apple Podcasts also creates transcripts for many episodes on iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can search them and tap a line to jump to that moment in the audio.

1. **Play the episode** in the Apple Podcasts app.
2. **Open the player** by tapping the episode at the bottom of the screen.
3. **Tap the Transcript button** (a speech bubble with a quotation mark) to show the text.
4. **Search the transcript** or tap any line to skip to it.

Like Spotify, Apple's transcript stays inside the app. It is also not available for every episode or language. To get an Apple podcast transcript you can edit and keep, use the episode's YouTube version or audio file with TranscriptX.

## How to Transcribe a Podcast From a YouTube Link

Many shows post full episodes on YouTube, especially video podcasts and interviews. That makes the YouTube link the fastest way to get a podcast transcript you can work with.

1. **Find the episode on YouTube** and copy its link. On a computer, click **Share** under the video, then **Copy**.
2. **Open [TranscriptX YouTube Transcript](/youtube-transcript/).**
3. **Paste the link** into the box that says *"Paste a YouTube video URL..."*.
4. **Click the arrow** (↑) to start. The title appears right away and the transcript fills in when it is ready.
5. **Read and fix the text.** Click **Edit** to correct names or terms. Each paragraph has a timestamp.
6. **Click Copy** to copy the transcript text, with timestamps.
7. **Or click Download**, the button next to Copy, and choose **.txt**, **.docx**, **.pdf**, **.srt** or **.vtt**.

TranscriptX writes the transcript from the audio, so it works even when the YouTube video has no captions. In our test, a four-minute YouTube video was transcribed in about six seconds. A full episode takes longer, but you do not have to type anything.

## How to Transcribe a Podcast Audio File (Podcast to Text)

If you have the episode as a file, you can turn the podcast to text directly. This is the method for your own show, for episodes you saved, and for raw interview recordings that never went online.

1. **Open [TranscriptX Audio to Text](/audio-to-text/).** For a video podcast file, use [Video to Text](/video-to-text/) instead.
2. **Click Upload** and choose where the file is: **From this device**, **Google Drive**, **Dropbox** or **OneDrive**.
3. **Pick the episode file**, such as an MP3, WAV or M4A.
4. **Click the arrow** (↑) to start transcribing.
5. **Check the transcript.** Play the audio from the player under the text and click **Edit** to fix anything that sounds off.
6. **Copy or download it.** **Copy** puts the text on your clipboard. **Download** opens the menu with **.txt**, **.docx**, **.pdf**, **.srt** and **.vtt**.

You do not need to know the language in advance. TranscriptX detects it automatically and supports more than 100 languages.

## How to Transcribe Your Own Podcast

If you host a show, a transcript does more than help you find quotes. It makes each episode readable for people who cannot or prefer not to listen, and it gives you text to reuse.

A simple workflow after you finish editing an episode:

1. **Export the final audio** from your editor as an MP3 or WAV.
2. **Upload it** to [Audio to Text](/audio-to-text/) and let it transcribe.
3. **Fix names and terms** with **Edit**. Guest names, brands and jargon are the usual suspects.
4. **Download what you need:**
   - **.docx** or **.pdf** for show notes and your website
   - **.srt** or **.vtt** for captions on the video version of the episode
   - **.txt** for pasting into a newsletter or a blog draft

**Who said what.** On the Pro plan, TranscriptX can label who is speaking, which helps with interviews and co-hosted shows. On the Free plan you get the full text without speaker labels.

**Record straight in the browser.** For a quick voice note or a solo episode draft, click **Record** instead of **Upload**. Keep the page open while recording, and the transcript appears when you finish.

## Tips for a Cleaner Podcast Transcript

AI transcription is fast, but the audio you give it still shapes the result. These habits make the text easier to use:

1. **Use the cleanest version of the file.** The final mixed episode usually transcribes better than a raw track with long silences.
1. **Expect music and crosstalk to be messy.** Intros with music under speech and people talking over each other are hard for any transcriber. Check those parts by ear.
1. **Correct names once, early.** Fix the spelling of each guest and brand the first time it appears, then search for the same mistake further down.
1. **Reduce background noise.** On the Pro plan, noise reduction helps with recordings made in busy rooms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my podcast not have a transcript on Spotify or Apple Podcasts?

Not every episode gets one. It depends on the show, the language and the creator's settings. If the app has none, transcribe the YouTube version or the audio file with TranscriptX.

### How long does it take to transcribe a podcast?

With AI, minutes rather than hours. Typing it by hand takes several times the length of the audio. See [how long it takes to transcribe 1 hour of audio](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-transcribe-1-hour-of-audio/) for the comparison.

### Is it legal to transcribe a podcast?

Transcribing an episode for your own notes or research is common practice. Publishing someone else's transcript is a different matter, so ask the show's owner first.

### Can I get a podcast transcript with speaker names?

Yes, on the Pro plan. TranscriptX labels who is speaking, and you can edit the names before you download.

### Can I turn a podcast transcript into subtitles?

Yes. Click **Download** and choose **.srt** or **.vtt** to get a timed subtitle file for the video version of your episode.

### Can ChatGPT transcribe a podcast?

Not from a link on its own. ChatGPT cannot listen to an episode you link to. You can [add TranscriptX to ChatGPT or Claude](/transcription-mcp/) so it gets the transcript first. Our guide on [whether ChatGPT can watch videos](/blog/can-chatgpt-watch-videos-here-s-how-to-make-it-read-them/) explains why.
