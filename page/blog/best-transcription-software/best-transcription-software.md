## 2. TurboScribe: best for bulk transcription of long files

TurboScribe is built for people with a pile of recordings. Its paid plan is sold as unlimited, with long files and many uploads at once, which makes it popular with podcasters, researchers and anyone digitizing archives.

### What TurboScribe does well

- Long files on the paid plan, up to 10 hours or 5GB each.
- Bulk uploads: up to 50 files at a time on the paid plan.
- Exports to .docx, .pdf, .txt and .srt.
- Translation of finished transcripts into 134+ languages.

### TurboScribe pricing

- **Free:** 3 files a day, each up to 30 minutes, one upload at a time and a slower queue.
- **Unlimited:** $20/month, or $10/month billed yearly ($120 a year).

### Good to know

The free plan is tight if your recordings run longer than half an hour. TurboScribe works with files you upload, so if most of your content lives on YouTube or social media, you'll need to download it first.

**Best for:** heavy users with many long audio files who want one flat monthly price.

## 3. Otter.ai: best for meeting notes

### Otter.ai pricing

## What to look for in transcription software

**Accuracy on your own audio.** Clean audio makes every tool look good. Try a recording with background noise, accents or several speakers, because that's where tools differ.

**How you get audio in.** If your content is on YouTube or social media, a tool that takes links saves you a download step every time. If you record calls, a meeting bot or live mode matters more.

**Export formats.** .txt and .docx are for reading and editing. .srt and .vtt are subtitle files for YouTube, video editors and course platforms. Check they're on the plan you'll actually use.

**What the free plan really covers.** Look at minutes per month, minutes per file and file-size limits. A free plan that stops at 30 minutes per file won't help with a two-hour lecture.

**Speaker labels.** For interviews and meetings, knowing who said what saves a lot of cleanup.

**Price per person.** Many tools charge per seat. If you work alone, a low flat price usually beats a "from" price that needs a yearly plan.
