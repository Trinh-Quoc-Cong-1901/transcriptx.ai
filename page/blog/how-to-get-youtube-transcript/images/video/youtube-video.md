How it works:
1. On YouTube, click Share, then Copy link.
2. Paste the link into TranscriptX's YouTube Transcript page and click the arrow.
3. Wait a few seconds. In our test, a 4-minute video was transcribed in about 6 seconds.
4. Click Copy to copy the whole transcript, timestamps included.
5. Or click Download, right next to Copy, to save it as TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT or VTT.

Timestamps can be turned on or off. It works in your phone's browser too, with the same steps.
