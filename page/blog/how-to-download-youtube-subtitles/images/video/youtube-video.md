Download subtitles from any YouTube video as an SRT or VTT file, in seconds. It works even when the video has no captions, because TranscriptX builds the subtitles from the audio.

📖 Full guide: https://transcriptx.ai/blog/how-to-download-youtube-subtitles/

How it works:
1. On YouTube, click Share, then Copy link.
2. Paste the link into YouTube Subtitle Downloader and click the arrow.
3. Wait a few seconds. In our test, a 4-minute video was done in about 6 seconds.
4. Click Download. It's the button next to Copy.
5. Pick .srt or .vtt to save a timed subtitle file, ready for VLC, video editors or your website player.

Want the text instead? The same menu has .txt, .docx and .pdf, and the Copy button copies the whole transcript.

Your own video? You can also download its captions from YouTube Studio > Subtitles. The guide above covers both ways, plus SRT vs VTT.
