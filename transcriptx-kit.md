## Details

**Tagline**
Free AI transcription for video, audio and social links in 100+ languages.

**Description**
TranscriptX is a browser-based AI transcription tool that turns any recording with speech into clean, punctuated text. Upload a video, audio file or voice memo, or paste a public link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Vimeo, X and more. It detects the language automatically across 100+ languages and exports to TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT or VTT. No install and no sign-up needed; signing in adds more daily credits and saves your history across devices. Built for creators, students, researchers, journalists and teams who need searchable text, captions or notes fast.

**Key features**
- Transcribe video, audio, voice recordings and public video links
- Works with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Vimeo, X and more
- 100+ languages with automatic language detection
- Export to TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT and VTT
- Optional timestamps for captions and subtitles
- Use inside ChatGPT and Claude via MCP

**Pros**
- Free daily use, no account required
- No download needed for social video links
- Ready-to-upload SRT/VTT caption files
- Files are auto-deleted within 12 hours; data not used for AI training

**Cons**
- Free plan has a daily credit limit
- Speaker labels and noise reduction require Pro
- Cannot transcribe private or login-only videos

**Use cases**
- Turn YouTube lectures and tutorials into study notes
- Generate subtitles for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram videos
- Transcribe interviews and podcasts for quotes and show notes
- Create meeting records from recorded calls
- Repurpose short-form video scripts into written content
